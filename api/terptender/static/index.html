Inventory, COAs, and lab-aware answers—so terpene chemistry actually shows up where products are sold.

Why dispensaries first

Land TerpTender in the dispensary—POS-adjacent tools, budtender workflows, or white-label chat. Once staff and shoppers expect accurate, batch-level terpene answers, the store must attach real COAs to SKUs. Those COAs already ship with the product from labs that tested the batch. TerpTender is the reason that data gets captured and used, not lost in a PDF folder.

Structured lab feeds (POST /imports/lab-results) close the loop; PDF vaulting still works for backfill. Everyone wins: dispensaries get coverage, brands get shelf clarity, labs see their full panels matter beyond compliance minimums.
